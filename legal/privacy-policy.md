# Privacy Policy — Career Chief

**Effective:** October 9, 2026
**Operator:** Career Chief, operated by Danny Jones (sole proprietor), Los Angeles, California
**Contact:** support@dannyjones.ai

Career Chief is an AI resume builder. This policy describes what information we handle and why. Short version: your resume is yours, we keep as little as possible, and we never see your payment details.

## 1. Information you give us

**Your resume and job text.** Everything you type or paste into Career Chief — your work history, skills, job descriptions — is processed in your browser. When you use an AI feature (drafting, research, tailoring), that text is sent to our server, which forwards it to the AI provider to generate the result for that request only. We do not keep a copy of your resume on our servers.

**Which AI company sees your text.** AI is strictly opt-in: each visit starts with AI off, and it only runs after you check the consent box. The consent box names the company that will receive your text — either **OpenAI** or **Anthropic (Claude)** — depending on which one is configured for the site. The company named there is the only AI company your content goes to for that request. AI is only enabled when a provider key is set; without one, the app analyzes what you provide locally and says so.

**What the research step does.** When you ask Career Chief to research a company, it uses the AI provider's web search. That means the company name, job title, and other terms from your materials are sent as search queries to the AI provider's search service. Public findings come back with clickable sources.

**AI processing notes.** Both providers state that API data is not used to train their models. For the current retention and abuse-monitoring terms of whichever provider is live, review their policy directly:
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

**Link reader.** If you paste a web address for the link reader, the URL is sent to our server, which fetches the page on your behalf. Our hosting provider processes that request in order to serve it.

## 2. Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. They collect your name, email address, and payment details and handle sales tax. **We never see or store your card number.** From a purchase we receive only your email address and your license status (which plan, whether the key is valid). See [Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy) for how they handle your data.

## 3. Stored in your browser

Your Pro license key is saved in your browser's local storage so you stay unlocked between visits. Clearing your browser data will remove it — you can re-enter your key anytime. We use no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.

## 4. What we don't do

- No user accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no profile to maintain.
- No analytics or advertising trackers.
- We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your resume content with anyone except the AI provider named in the consent box, for the sole purpose of generating your requested results.

## 5. Your rights

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you (for example, the email address associated with a license), email support@dannyjones.ai. If you are a California resident, this policy also serves as our notice of collection under the CCPA/CPRA: the categories above are what we collect, and we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined.

## 6. Children

Career Chief is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## 7. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, keep license validation server-side where it matters, and minimize what we retain — the data we don't keep can't leak. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects how your data is handled, we'll update this page and note the new effective date at the top. Continued use of Career Chief after changes means you accept the updated policy.
