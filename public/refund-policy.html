If you're not happy with Career Chief Pro, email support@dannyjones.ai within 14 days of purchase and we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked, no forms. Refunds are processed by Lemon Squeezy back to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days.

After the window

Refund requests after the window are handled case by case. If the product genuinely doesn't work for you (a technical problem we can't fix, for example), reach out — we'd rather make it right than keep your money.

What this doesn't cover

Chargebacks

If something's wrong, email us first. A chargeback freezes the process for everyone and is slower than a direct refund — we'll sort it out faster by email.
